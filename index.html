Characters

LITTLE MY

Little My is a tiny, fearless girl known for her high bun and red dress. She is brutally honest and loves causing chaos, but she is also deeply loyal to her friends.

MOOMINMAMMA

Moominmamma is the calm, loving heart of the Moomin family, always ready with a warm meal, comforting words, and solution for any problem hidden inside her famous black handbag.

MOOMINPAPPA

Moominpappa is adventurous and loves telling stories about his past. He is proud of his family and enjoys writing his memoirs. Recognizable by his black top hat and walking stick.
